The Swagger Trail: Your Map for the Next 4 Months

Every good trail starts with a map at the trailhead — one of those weathered wooden signs with a little red dot that says You Are Here. This is that sign.

For the last two posts I’ve been telling you why your walk matters. Starting to-day, we actually start walking. So before we do, here’s the whole route, top to bottom, so you know where we’re headed and how long the hike is.

Open the Swagger Trail map: https://thehospitalhippie-lab.github.io/swagger-trail/map/

The trail runs down your body

My father says: where the head goes, the body will follow. So that’s where we start — at the top — and we work our way down, one body part at a time, until we’re standing on our toes. Each stop is named after a question we already ask ourselves in plain American English, because the language figured this out long before I did.

Stop 1 — Head: Is my head on straight? Brain waves, attention as the greatest currency we’ve got, why multitasking lies to you, and the first exercise that starts everything.

Stop 2 — Shoulder: Do I have a chip on my shoulder? Grudges, gaslighting, road rage, and how to finally set the chip down.

Stop 3 — Heart: Is my heart in the right place? Bystanders, head people versus heart people versus gut people, and the breathing that settles a racing chest.

Stop 4 — Arms: Am I swinging for the fences? Reaching further than feels safe, what actually makes something funny, and patting yourself on the back like you mean it.

Stop 5 — Belly: Am I yellow-bellied? Fear, food, eleven laughs a day, and the bodily habits nobody wants to talk about.

Stop 6 — Hips: Is it all in the hips? Hula, hip isolation, dancing, and getting dressed backward on purpose.

Stop 7 — Legs & Stride: Can I shake a leg? Decisions that won’t grow on trees, eating the frog, and taking it all in stride for the long haul.

Summit — Toes: Will I tiptoe, toe the line, or go toe-to-toe? Your shadow fight, the posture that already told everyone about you, the real timeline of change, and your 9-step Swagger action plan.

Three legs of the hike

The book splits the trail into three parts, and you’ll feel the terrain change as we go:

Part One — Radical Self-Acceptance & Confrontational Healing. Head, shoulder, heart. This is the mental hygiene leg: what’s going on upstairs, and what you’re still carrying.

Part Two — Reintegrating Nervous Tension Between Mind and Spirit with Bodily Reminders. Arms, belly, hips. This is where it gets physical. More exercises, more moving, more “did he really just write a post about that?”

Part Three — Self-Development & Stress Management. Legs, stride, toes. The long walk home: decisions, persistence, and choosing how you’ll carry yourself from here.

How to walk it with me

New stops go up every Tuesday and Thursday. Every new stop also lands in your inbox the same day it goes up.

Each stop comes with exercises from the book, fifty-three of them in all. Some take thirty seconds. Some will feel ridiculous. Do them anyway. Nobody gets anything out of reading a trail map from the parking lot.

If you join late, no problem — every post is numbered by its stop, so you can start at the Trailhead and catch up at your own pace. Save the map. Screenshot it. Mark where you are.

You are here. Let’s walk.
